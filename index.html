Testimonials Block: Introduction

A happy customer's own words sell better than anything you could write. Testimonials Block gives you one flexible block with 39 ready-made layouts, so visitors actually see them.

Whatever reviews you already have, you can turn them into a layout without retyping a word.

What You Get

  • 39 layouts that share the same reviews and the same settings sidebar.
  • A Testimonials post type, so you write a review once and reuse it anywhere.
  • A front-end form that saves visitor reviews as pending, so you check them first.
  • Score badges styled after Google, Trustpilot, G2, Facebook and Capterra, plus a verified buyer seal.
  • A Feedback & NPS Poll block with its own results screen.
  • Responsive controls for columns and gaps on desktop, tablet and mobile.
  • Search engine stars. The plugin adds the data Google needs to show review stars. There is nothing to switch on, and Google decides whether to show them.
  • Import tools for other plugins, spreadsheets, and your Google and Facebook reviews.

Where to Go Next

Start with the Quick Start. You will publish your first section in about five minutes. Not installed yet? See Installation & Activation.

Installation & Activation

Installing Testimonials Block works like any other plugin. There is no licence key and no setup wizard.

Requirements

  • WordPress 6.5 or newer.
  • PHP 7.4 or newer.
  • The block editor (Gutenberg), which ships with WordPress.
  • Any current version of Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge.
Note

Only building the section needs the block editor. Visitors need nothing special, and it works on classic and block themes.

Install from the WordPress Dashboard

  1. Go to Plugins > Add New Plugin.
  2. Search for Testimonials Block.
  3. Click Install Now on that listing, then click Activate on the Installed Plugins screen.

Install by Uploading the ZIP

  1. Download b-testimonials-block.zip from the plugin page on WordPress.org.
  2. Go to Plugins > Add New Plugin > Upload Plugin and choose the file.
  3. Click Install Now, then click Activate on the same row.

You can also copy the unzipped folder to /wp-content/plugins/ over FTP and activate it from the Plugins screen.

What Happens on Activation

Your first activation takes you straight to the Testimonials screen. A Testimonials menu then appears in the admin sidebar with nine items.

  • All Testimonials: your reviews. See Managing Testimonials in One Place.
  • Add New Testimonial: write a new review.
  • Categories: groups used by the Filter & Search panel. See Filter and Search Your Reviews.
  • Shortcode: a saved layout with a shortcode. See Using the Blocks Elsewhere.
  • Submissions: visitor reviews waiting for approval. See Reviewing Customer Submissions.
  • Feedback & NPS Poll: poll results. See Feedback & NPS Poll Results.
  • Demo & Help: live demos and block on/off switches. See Demo & Help Dashboard.
  • Import / Export: bring reviews in or out as CSV. See Bringing In Testimonials From Elsewhere.
  • Review Sources: connect Google and Facebook. See Connecting Google and Facebook Reviews.

Quick Start: Your First Section

Start with a blank page, end with a published wall of reviews. It takes about five minutes and no code.

A Faster Start: Ready-Made Patterns

A pattern is a finished section with heading, layout and sample reviews. Insert one, then swap in your own words.

  1. Click the + button and open the Patterns tab.
  2. Choose Testimonials & Social Proof from the categories.
  3. Pick one and click it to insert the whole section.

The seven patterns: Wall of Love, SaaS Hero with Rating, E-commerce Social Proof, Agency Results & Testimonials, Review Platform Trust Bar, Scrolling Testimonial Marquee and Ask for a Review.

Tip

Sample reviews are ignored by Automatic Search Engine Markup until you rewrite them as your own.

Prefer an empty block? Continue with Step 1.

Step 1 - Insert the Testimonials Block

  1. Go to Pages > Add Page (older WordPress calls it Add New Page), or open an existing post or page.
  2. Click the + button and search for Testimonials, or type /testimonials in an empty paragraph and press Enter.
  3. The block lands on the canvas with a layout picker, not a finished design.

You find the block under the bPlugins category in the inserter.

Step 2 - Pick a Layout

The picker is headed Select Your Testimonial Block. Click a card to use that layout.

If none fits, click View All 40+ Layouts & Widgets in Modal Popup, or Use Classic Single Block Slider to keep the original single block.

Tip

Pick anything for now. Nothing you type is tied to the layout, so you can change it later.

Switching Layouts Later

The block switcher changes the layout without retyping a review. It is the fastest way to try the whole library.

  1. Select the block and open Select / Switch Block at the top of the General tab.
  2. Click Change Block / Layout.
  3. A window called Testimonial Block Switcher opens. Search for a layout, or use the category chips. Then click Use This Layout on a card.

Two buttons at the foot do something different. Switch to 40+ Layout Placeholder empties the block back to the picker. Switch to Classic Single Block Mode returns to the original single block.

Your review cards, photos, ratings, Content Source and badge text survive a switch. Settings only one layout has do not, but nothing is deleted. Switch back and they return.

Tip

A layout switched off on the All Blocks screen does not appear in the switcher. See Turning Blocks On and Off.

Step 3 - Enter Your Reviews

Every layout arrives with sample cards. Replace them on the General tab, because any you leave behind get published too. See A Block Shows Sample Reviews on the Front End.

  1. Open Add or Remove Testimonial Cards and click a card chip to open it.
  2. Fill in Name, Designation, Review and Rating, and upload an Image for the photo.
  3. Click Add New Card for the next review, or Duplicate to copy the one you just wrote.
  4. Or click +, Add New Item, in the block toolbar above the block, to add a card without leaving the canvas.
  5. Click Remove on any card you do not need.

Reusing the same reviews in several places? Set Content Source to Testimonials (CPT). See Content Source and Review Cards.

Step 4 - Fit It to Your Design

  • On the General tab, set Columns, Column Gap and Row Gap in the Layout panel.
  • On the Style tab, set the card background, border and shadow in the Card panel.
  • Set fonts and colours for the name, designation and review in their own panels.

You only see controls that change something on your layout. See The Style Tab: Cards and Photos.

Step 5 - Publish and Check

  1. Click Publish or Update.
  2. Open the page on the front end.
  3. Resize the browser window, or use the editor's tablet and mobile previews, to check your column counts.
Tip

If you use a caching plugin such as WP Rocket or LiteSpeed Cache, clear the cache after publishing. Otherwise the page may keep serving old markup.

Success Check

You'll know it worked when, on the published page:

  • Every review shows its photo, name, designation, text and stars.
  • The columns match what you set for your device.
  • Sliders move, marquees scroll, and Expand opens a truncated review.

Managing Testimonials in One Place

Write a review once and use it everywhere. The Testimonials post type holds all your reviews, so no block needs its own copy.

Add a Testimonial

  1. Go to Testimonials > Add New.
  2. Put the reviewer's name in the title field.
  3. Put the review itself in the main content area.
  4. Fill in Rating, Designation and Company in the Testimonial Details box. It sits on the right, or below the editor on a narrow screen.
  5. Set a Featured image for the reviewer's photo.
  6. Click Publish.
Note

Testimonials open in the classic editor on purpose. You see the title, the review and three short fields all at once.

The Testimonial Details Fields

These are the three fields in the Testimonial Details box.

  • Rating (0-5): the star rating. Half stars such as 4.5 work. Default: 5.
  • Designation: the line under the name, for example Head of Product.
  • Company: saved with the review. Blocks that show one line under the name use Designation.
Note

Versions before 1.0.5 rounded half stars down to a whole star. If you still see that, update the plugin.

The Testimonials List

The All Testimonials list adds columns next to the title, so you can scan your reviews without opening them.

  • Designation, as you typed it.
  • Rating, drawn as stars.
  • Testimonial Categories, the groups a review belongs to. See Filter and Search Your Reviews.
  • Classic Shortcode, a copyable [testimonial id=N] tag for one review. See Embed One Review.

Testimonials are not public pages. They only exist to feed your blocks.

Use Them in a Block

Insert any layout and set Content Source to Testimonials (CPT). It fills itself from this list and stays current as you add reviews. See Content Source and Review Cards.

Bringing In Testimonials From Elsewhere

You probably have reviews somewhere already. Testimonials > Import / Export can bring them in from another plugin, a CSV file, or Google and Facebook, so you never retype them.

From Another Plugin

The screen finds Strong Testimonials, Real Testimonials, Site Reviews and WooCommerce product reviews on its own. This works even if that plugin is deactivated.

  1. Go to Testimonials > Import / Export.
  2. Under Import from another plugin, find the source in the table.
  3. Click Import. Later, the button reads Check for new and brings in only new reviews.

Names, job titles, companies, ratings, photos and dates come across. Nothing in the other plugin is changed, and photos are not uploaded twice.

From a CSV File

Columns are matched by name, so a spreadsheet from almost anywhere should work as it is. These headings are recognised:

  • Name: Name, Author, Client or Reviewer.
  • Review: Review, Text, Testimonial or Feedback.
  • Number: Rating, Stars or Score.

Job title, company, photo address and date columns are picked up the same way. A file over 5 MB is refused, so split a very large one in two.

Note

A file with no recognised name or review column is refused with an explanation, not half imported.

Under Export, the same screen saves every testimonial as a CSV. Use it for a backup or a move to another site. Published, draft, pending and private ones are all included.

From Google and Facebook

Once a platform is connected on Review Sources, this screen can pull in the written reviews, not just the score. See Import Your Past Reviews as Testimonials.

Content Source and Review Cards

Your reviews come from the block itself or from the Testimonials post type. You can switch whenever you like. Both options sit at the top of the General tab.

Which One to Use

  • Manual: reviews for one page only, written inside the block.
  • Testimonials (CPT): reviews reused across the site, in lists that update themselves.

Switch to the Post Type

Use this when you want one set of reviews to feed many blocks.

  1. Select the block and open the General tab.
  2. Open the Content Source panel.
  3. Set Source to Testimonials (CPT).
  4. Pick a Category if the block should show one group, then set Number, Order By and Order.
  • Category: one category only. Default: All categories.
  • Number: how many to load, up to 100. Default: 6.
  • Order By: Date, Title or Menu Order. Default: Date.
  • Order: Descending or Ascending. Default: Descending.

Only published testimonials appear. Switching back to Manual brings your cards back exactly as they were.

Note

Editing a testimonial updates every block that uses it. You do not need to open those pages.

Show One Category Per Block

Say you want each year of reviews in its own section. Put each testimonial in a category like 2026, 2025 and 2024, then add one block per year.

  1. Add the block and set Source to Testimonials (CPT).
  2. In Content Source, pick the year from Category. The list shows how many testimonials each category holds.
  3. Repeat with a second block for the next year.

A block pinned to a category loads only that category, so the page stays light. Number counts only that category. If you delete the category later, the block shows nothing and marks it as not found.

Category or Category filter?

Category sets what the block holds. The Category filter in Filter & Search adds buttons so visitors can switch. Use Category for separate sections, the filter for one block with everything.

Show a Few, Then More

Fifty cards make a very long page. The Show More panel shows the first few, with a button that reveals the next batch.

  1. Select the block and open the General tab.
  2. Open Show More and switch Show more button on.
  3. Set Show at first (cards shown on load) and Show per click (cards added each click).
  4. Change Button text if you do not want "Show more".

The panel appears on layouts that stack cards down the page: grids, lists, masonry, the timeline, and the audio and video lists. Sliders and the marquee do not have it.

Note

The button only reveals testimonials the block has loaded. With Testimonials (CPT), set Number to the total you want, for example 30, and Show at first to 6.

It works with the filter bar and search box. The button disappears once everything is showing. With hand-typed cards, every card stays visible in the editor, and the button appears on the live page.

Style the Show More Button

Once Show more button is on, a Show More Button panel appears on the Style tab. Use it to match your site.

  • Text Color, Background and Border Color: the normal look.
  • Hover Text Color and Hover Background: the look under the mouse. The default is the accent color with white text.
  • Font Size (px), Roundness (px) and Border Width (px): 0 gives square corners or no outline. Empty roundness is fully round.
  • Button Width (px): leave empty and the button fits its text.
  • Space Above (px) and Alignment: the gap above the button, and left, center or right.

Every setting is optional. Empty ones keep the original look.

A Note on Menu Order

Order By offers Menu Order, but the testimonial editor has no order field. So every testimonial falls back to date order.

To set the sequence yourself, use Manual cards and drag them into place. Or name your testimonials so Title order gives the sequence you want.

Tip

On a slider or marquee, set Number higher than you need. A longer list means less obvious repetition.

Filter and Search Your Reviews

Thirty reviews are easier to read when visitors can narrow them down. The Filter & Search panel adds category buttons, a search box, or both, with no page reload.

Group Reviews Into Categories

Categories belong to the Testimonials post type. Set Content Source to Testimonials (CPT) first.

  1. Go to Testimonials > Categories and add your groups, for example by product line or service.
  2. Open each testimonial and assign it to one or more categories.
  3. On the block, open Filter & Search and switch Category filter on.
  4. Set the "All" button text if you want something other than the default.

A category with no testimonials never shows a button.

Style the Buttons

Once Category filter is on, a Filter Bar panel appears on the Style tab. Change as much or as little as you like.

  • Button Colors: tabs for Normal, Hover and Selected (the picked category). Each has a text color, background and border color.
  • Button Text: font size, weight, letter case and letter spacing.
  • Button Shape: padding, border width (0 removes the outline) and roundness (0 is square).
  • Button Layout: left, center or right, and the space between buttons.
  • Spacing: the gap between the bar and the testimonials.

Every setting is optional. Empty ones keep the original look. The selected button uses the accent color with white text.

Tip

Until you pick a Normal color, buttons look slightly faded so they read well on light and dark backgrounds. Any Normal color shows them at full strength.

Add a Search Box

Switch Search box on to search the name, role, company and review text. Set Search placeholder to change the hint text.

Style the Search Box

Once Search box is on, a Search Box panel appears on the Style tab, just under Filter Bar.

  • Colors: Normal sets text, placeholder, background and border. When Typing sets the background and border after a visitor clicks in.
  • Text and Shape: font size, padding, border width (0 removes the outline) and roundness (0 is square).
  • Size and Position: switch on Full width, or set a width. Position puts the box next to the buttons by default, or on its own row.

With the category buttons off, you also get Space Below the Box. Every setting is optional.

Tip

Filtering only changes what is on screen. Every testimonial still counts toward the structured data Google reads. See Automatic Search Engine Markup.

What It Looks Like

The bar sits right above your reviews, in the editor and on the live page. All is selected first, then one button for each category that has a review.

The Card Editor

With Content Source set to Manual, your reviews live in the Add or Remove Testimonial Cards panel. Click a chip, like Card 1, to open that card.

Two layouts rename the panel: Add or Remove Case Study Cards on Customer Case Study, and Single Testimonial Settings on one-review layouts.

  • Image: the reviewer's photo. Upload a file or paste a URL.
  • Name: the reviewer's name.
  • Designation: the line under the name.
  • Review: the review text.
  • Rating: stars, from 0 to 5.

Some layouts add fields. Audio Testimonials adds Audio File. Customer Case Study adds Challenge, Solution and Result.

You do not have to fill every field. The Elements panel switches card parts on or off for the whole block. See The General Tab.

Add, Copy and Remove

  • Add New Card: adds an empty card at the end.
  • Duplicate: copies the open card, with its photo and rating.
  • Remove: deletes the open card.
Tip

Build one card the way you want it, then Duplicate it and change only the text.

Single-Widget Blocks

Sixteen blocks show one widget, not a list of cards, so they have no card editor. Their text comes from a panel named after the block, like Google Review Badge Settings or Poll Settings.

  • Badges and seals: the five score badges, the verified buyer seal, the floating review badge and Trust Badges.
  • Summaries: Rating Summary, Star Rating Progress Bars and Testimonial Stats.
  • Interactive: the Testimonial Form, the Feedback & NPS Poll, the Before / After slider, the Comparison Review Table and the FAQ Review Accordion.

Reviewing Customer Submissions

A visitor fills in the Testimonial Form and clicks submit. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

The Submissions Screen

Go to Testimonials > Submissions. A count bubble shows on the menu when something is waiting. Other admin screens show a notice with a View Submissions button.

You can dismiss the notice. It comes back only when a new submission arrives. The count bubble clears when the queue is empty.

Four filters group the queue:

  • Pending Review: new submissions waiting for you. This is the default view.
  • Approved: submissions you published.
  • Trash: submissions you rejected.
  • All Submissions: pending and approved together, without the trash.
Note

Approved and All also list testimonials you added by hand. Only Pending is a true queue of visitor submissions.

Approve, Reject or Delete

Three actions cover every row.

  • Approve: publishes the review. Any block set to Testimonials (CPT) can now show it.
  • Reject: moves it to the trash, where you can still recover it.
  • Delete Permanently: removes it for good. This cannot be undone.

To act on several at once, tick the rows and use the bulk action select. It offers Approve & Publish and Move to Trash. In Trash, it offers Delete Permanently.

Four tiles above the filters count Pending, Approved, In Trash and Total.

What a Submission Contains

The form saves what the visitor typed as a normal testimonial. Their name becomes the title and their review becomes the content.

Note

You can edit a submission before you approve it. Open it from All Testimonials, fix a typo, then publish.

Important

The form accepts anonymous submissions, because customers are not logged in. Check the queue often, and read every review before you approve it.

Connecting Google and Facebook Reviews

Connect your Google and Facebook accounts once. After that, your badges and review blocks show your real star rating and real reviews by themselves. No code needed.

What This Page Is For

Each badge block has a Rating Source setting. It decides where the stars and text come from.

  • Live from ... (the default): shows your real rating from Google or Facebook. You set this up on this page.
  • Manual (this block only): shows the number and text you type in, for that one block.

Until you connect, a badge set to Live shows a placeholder example.

Where to Find This Page

In your WordPress admin, click Testimonials, then Review Sources. You will see one card for Google and one for Facebook.

What about Trustpilot, G2 and Capterra

They have no card here yet. Trustpilot and G2 only allow automatic reading on a paid plan, and Capterra does not allow it. Type their ratings in by hand. See Typing In Reviews by Hand.

Connect Google Reviews

The Google card needs two things from the free Google Cloud Console. A Place ID tells Google which business you mean. An API key works like a password for your review data.

  1. Open the Google Cloud Console API Library and sign in.
  2. Click Select a project in the top bar.
  3. Pick an existing project, or create a new one.
  4. Search for Places API and press Enter. Click Places API (New). The plain Places API will not work.
  5. Click the blue Enable button.
    You may be asked to add billing first

    Google asks for this before any API, even free ones. Open Google's billing setup page and click Create Billing Account. See "About cost" below.

  6. Click APIs & Services, then Credentials.
  7. Click + Create Credentials, then API key.
  8. Copy the key and paste it into API key on the Google card.
  9. Click the key to open it. Under API restrictions, choose Restrict key and allow only Places API (New). Leave Application restrictions on None. Do not pick HTTP referrers (websites), because the key runs on your server and that would break it.
  10. Open Google's Place ID Finder. Type your business name and click it on the map to see your Place ID, like ChIJN1t_tDeuEmsRUsoyG83frY4.
  11. Paste it into Place ID on the Google card.
  12. Click Save, then Test & fetch now. Your real rating, review count and up to five reviews appear on the card.
About cost

Normal use costs nothing. The plugin checks about once a day for your whole site, far below Google's free monthly amount. See Google's pricing page.

Connect Facebook Reviews

The Facebook card has two ways to connect. Try the easy one first.

Way 1: Click "Connect with Facebook" (the easy way)

  1. Click Connect with Facebook. A popup shows Facebook's login.
  2. Log in with the account that manages your business Page and allow access.
  3. If you manage several Pages, choose the right one.
  4. The popup closes and the page reloads. You now see a green CONNECTED label and a Disconnect Facebook button.

You never copy any secret codes. The plugin handles them for you.

Way 2: Type in a Page access token yourself (for advanced users only)

Two boxes below the button take your details directly.

  • Page ID: the number or username of your Page.
  • Page access token: it must be a long-lived token. A short-lived one stops after a couple of hours and your badge quietly shows old numbers.
Tip

Use Way 1 if you can. The usual mistake with Way 2 is pasting a personal User token. Only a Page token can read your Page's reviews.

One thing Facebook needs, first

Your Page must have Recommendations turned on, with at least one recommendation. Otherwise neither way has anything to show.

Reading the Status Card

After you connect, the card shows what is happening.

  • A score, stars and a number: your real data.
  • "fetched 2 hours ago": when the plugin last checked. It checks every 12 hours by default.
  • A red message: the last check failed and it says why. Often a wrong code, a wrong ID, or a Page with no recommendations.
  • Test & fetch now: checks right away. Click it after any change to the card.

Choose How Often Ratings Refresh

The Refresh Window card sets how long a rating is reused before the plugin asks again. Pick 1 to 168 hours. The default of 12 suits almost every site.

  1. Go to Testimonials > Review Sources and scroll to Refresh Window.
  2. Type the number of hours in the Hours box.
  3. Click Save Settings.

Visitors never wait for a refresh. A shorter window shows new reviews sooner but asks Google or Facebook more often. Ratings you typed by hand ignore this setting.

When a Platform Stops Answering

If a badge cannot reach Google or Facebook, it shows the last good figure. Then it shows the average of your own testimonials, then a placeholder. That keeps the page tidy, but a broken connection looks just like a working one.

So the plugin warns you in two places.

  • An admin notice on the plugin's screens, the Dashboard and the Plugins list. It names the platform and the reason.
  • A Site Health test, marked recommended, not critical.

Fix the problem and the notice goes away. A new failure brings it back. On Facebook, click Reconnect with Facebook to get working again in one click.

Import Your Past Reviews as Testimonials

A connected badge shows a live score. You can also import the written reviews as testimonials, so any of the 39 layouts can show them.

  1. Connect Google or Facebook above, if you have not already.
  2. Go to Testimonials > Import / Export.
  3. Under Import from Google and Facebook, click Import for your platform.

It reuses the credentials already on this page. Running it again only adds new reviews.

  • Google gives at most five reviews per listing.
  • Facebook recommendations with no text are skipped.
  • Google reviews keep the language the customer wrote in.
Note

A Google Cloud project made since 1 March 2025 can only use the newer endpoint. Leave Which Google endpoint to read on Automatic, or pick one if you know which your key supports.

Showing Your Connected Reviews in a Block

Add a Google or Facebook badge block to a page or post. The Floating Review Badge asks which platform to use. Then open the Rating Source panel.

  • Rating Source: Live from Google (or Facebook) is already selected. A small box shows it is connected, when it was last checked, and a Refresh now button.
  • Display as lets you pick Rating badge (score, stars and count) or Review quotes (written reviews as cards).
Note

Not connected yet? Nothing breaks. The block shows an example rating and a note pointing back to this page.

Showing the Actual Written Reviews

Set Display as to Review quotes to show the real words your customers wrote. These settings control the look.

  • Show reviewer avatar: their photo, or their first letter in a circle.
  • Wrap the text in quotation marks: adds quote marks.
  • Quote text length: cuts long reviews and adds a Read more link, so cards stay the same height.
  • Reviews to show: how many appear if you have not picked any.
  • Minimum rating to include: hides lower ratings. Use 0 to show all.
  • Link "See more reviews" to the profile: links to your Google or Facebook page.

Set card width and height in Width & Height under the Style tab.

Picking Exactly Which Reviews to Show

Want the same reviews every time? Use Choose which to show. Leave every box unchecked and the plugin shows the top few. Check some and only those show, in the order you checked them.

With more than eight reviews, a search box appears to help you find one.

Note

If a review you picked is later deleted, the plugin goes back to showing the top few and tells you with a small note.

One Thing Facebook Does Differently

Facebook stopped star ratings for Pages in 2018. Customers now give a plain Recommendation, a yes or a no.

So Facebook cards show Recommended or Not Recommended instead of stars. Old star ratings from before 2018 still show as stars.

Note

A Facebook reviewer's name and photo show on the card, but the name is not a link. Facebook gives no public profile link.

Typing In Reviews by Hand

You can skip live data and type reviews yourself. It works for every platform and needs no connection.

  1. Set Rating Source to Manual (this block only).
  2. Set Display as to Review quotes.
  3. Click Add New Quote and fill in the fields below.

Trustpilot, G2 and Capterra badges have no Rating Source choice, since typing is their only option. Just set Display as to Review quotes. On the Floating Review Badge, set Rating Source to Manual, then pick a platform in the Platform list.

  • Reviewer Photo (optional): an image link, or upload one.
  • Reviewer Name: who left the review.
  • Rating: 0 to 5 stars.
  • Review Text: the words of the review.
  • When (optional): short text shown as typed, like "2 weeks ago". Copy it from the real review.

If Something Goes Wrong

  • Google shows "request denied" or 403. Turn on Places API (New) for your project and attach a billing account.
  • Google worked, then stopped with the same error. Your key may be limited to HTTP referrers. Set Application restrictions to None and limit API restrictions to Places API (New).
  • Facebook shows 0 reviews or a "page access token" error. You saved a User token. Use Connect with Facebook instead.
  • Facebook shows no star rating. Recommendations are off, or nobody has left one yet.
  • A badge shows an old number. Click Test & fetch now on that platform's card.

Say in the success message that you read submissions before publishing. It sets expectations and stops people submitting twice.

  1. Find it under Testimonials > Submissions, where the submenu shows a count. See Reviewing Customer Submissions.

Tip

Pick the theme before you tune typography. Badges and the timeline have no theme.

These three controls are saved per device:

  • Columns: Cards per row on desktop, tablet and mobile. Starts at 3, 2 and 1.

Cards show in the order of the card editor. With Testimonials (CPT), use Order By and Order instead.

The Slider Panel

This panel shows for Slider, 3D Slider and Coverflow.

  • AutoPlay: Moves the slider on its own. On.

  • Loop: Returns to the first slide. On.
  • MouseWheel: Lets the wheel move it. On.
  • Navigation: Shows arrows. On.

These show for Coverflow and 3D Slider only.

This panel shows for the Marquee arrangement.

Size, Spacing and Responsive

Width & Height sizes the block and its cards. Spacing sets the room around them. Both are saved per device.

Width and Height

  • Block Width: How wide the block is. Accepts px, % and em. Starts at the full column.
  • Card Height: Gives every card one height. Starts as follows content.
  • Alignment: Default, Left, Center or Right, for a block narrower than its column.

Alignment shows only when the block is narrower than its column, such as the review badges or a smaller Block Width.

In Review quotes mode, this panel adds Card Width. Left empty, cards use 220px.

Spacing

  • Block Margin: Space around the whole block, per side.
  • Card Margin: Space around each card, per side.

Space inside a card is Padding in the Card panel. Space between cards is Column Gap and Row Gap in the Layout panel.

Tip

In a grid, use gaps instead of card margins. Gaps keep columns even. Margins add up and can push the last column out of line.

The Device Switcher

Per-device panels show a device icon that follows the editor preview.

  1. Switch the editor preview to Tablet or Mobile from the top toolbar.
  2. Open the panel you want to change. Its device icon now shows that device.
  3. Set the value. It applies to that device only.
  4. Switch back to Desktop to check nothing there moved.

A Sensible Starting Point

  • Desktop: Three columns for short reviews, two for long ones.
  • Tablet: Two columns and a smaller column gap.
  • Mobile: One column, a smaller row gap, and a review font one or two points smaller.
Tip

Check sliders and marquees on mobile before you publish. A card width that suits desktop can leave a phone showing half a card.

What Switching Off Does

  • The layout leaves the block switcher modal and the layout picker.
  • Its demo link disappears from the Demos page.
  • It can no longer be inserted.
Important

A page that already uses a switched-off block will not show it. Switch it back on, or replace it, before you leave it off.

The One Block You Cannot Switch Off

The parent Testimonials block has a red Required badge and a greyed out switch. Every layout is its child, so Deactivate All skips it too.

Tip

New blocks in a future release arrive switched on, so an update never hides a new layout from you.

Settings and Uninstall Data

Decide one thing here: if you delete the plugin, should your reviews go with it or stay behind?

The Uninstall Toggle

  1. Go to Testimonials > Demo & Help and open Settings.
  2. Set the delete-data-on-uninstall toggle the way you want it. It is off to begin with.
  3. Switching it off saves straight away. Switching it on asks you to type DELETE to confirm, then it saves.
  • Off (the default): your testimonials, submissions, poll votes and settings stay in the database.
  • On: testimonials in every state, form photos, poll votes, NPS band settings and the block on/off list are all removed.

Deleting the plugin always disconnects your Google and Facebook credentials and clears cached scores and reviews. Reconnect from Review Sources if you reinstall.

Important

With the toggle on, deleting the plugin also deletes visitor-uploaded photos. Take a database backup first.

Deactivating Is Safe

Deactivating changes nothing. The clean-up only runs when you delete the plugin with the toggle on.

Tip

Leave the toggle off unless you are certain.

Feedback & NPS Poll Results

Every vote from a Feedback & NPS Poll block stays on your own site. This screen sums them up, and nothing is sent elsewhere.

Read the Screen

Go to Testimonials > Feedback & NPS Poll. The submenu shows the total votes so far.

Four figures sit across the top:

  • Net promoter score: the share of promoters minus the share of detractors, as a whole number.
  • Total marks selected: how many people answered.
  • Average selected mark: the mean of every mark given, against the top of your scale.
  • Promoter percentage: the share of answers in the promoter band.

Below them, Selected Marks Breakdown draws one bar per mark, highest to lowest, with its vote count and percentage.

The Three Bands

The score is built from three bands. These are the defaults on a 0 to 10 scale.

  • Promoter: counted in full towards the score. Marks 9 and above.
  • Passive: counted in the total, not in the score. Marks 7 and 8.
  • Detractor: subtracted from the score. Marks below 7.

Move the two thresholds and rename the bands on this screen, then save. Reset puts the standard values back.

Clear the Votes

Running a test or starting a new survey period? Clear votes whenever you like.

  • Delete a single vote from its row in the list.
  • Use Clear All Responses, at the top of the screen, to empty the whole record.
Important

Clearing cannot be undone, and the plugin keeps only the most recent 5,000 votes. Note the score first if you want to compare later.

Using the Blocks Elsewhere

Two shortcodes put a review or a whole layout anywhere a shortcode runs: a classic page, a widget, most page builders or a theme template.

Two Shortcodes, Two Jobs

  • [testimonial id=N] shows one review in the default layout. Copy it from the Classic Shortcode column on All Testimonials.
  • [testimonials_block id=N] shows a whole saved block, any of the 39 layouts. Copy it from the Block Shortcode column on Testimonials > Shortcode.

Embed One Review

Every testimonial gets a shortcode once it exists. Write the review first, then take its tag.

  1. Go to Testimonials > Add New Testimonial.
  2. Add the reviewer's name as the title, the review text below it, and the sidebar's Rating, Designation and Company. Publish it.
  3. Go to Testimonials > All Testimonials.
  4. Find the Classic Shortcode column and click the tag for your review, for example [testimonial id=2591]. It is a click-to-copy button.
  5. Open the page or post. Click the + button, search for Shortcode and add the core Shortcode block.
  6. Paste the copied tag into the empty box.
  7. Publish and view the page. The shortcode is replaced by that one review.

An unpublished review follows the usual WordPress rules. Visitors see nothing until you publish it.

Embed a Whole Layout

For a full grid, slider or any other layout outside the block editor, build it once and take the shortcode it gives you.

  1. Go to Testimonials > Shortcode and click Add New Testimonials Block.
  2. Give it a title. This is the only way to tell it apart from your other saved layouts later. Then pick a layout and set its Content Source as you would on a page.
  3. Publish it. The banner's copy icon also copies the tag, without leaving the editor.
  4. Copy the tag from the Block Shortcode column, for example [testimonials_block id=2788]. Click the blue tag itself. It is a click-to-copy button.
  5. Open the page or post. Click the + button, search for Shortcode and add the core Shortcode block.
  6. Paste the copied [testimonials_block id=N] tag into the empty box.
  7. The tag stays plain text in the editor. It turns into the layout on the front end.
  8. Publish the page and view it. The shortcode is replaced by your layout.
Note

This post is configuration, not content. It has no front-end page or slug of its own.

First write and publish your reviews. See Embed One Review.

  1. In the page list, hover the page and click Edit with Elementor.
  2. In the Elements panel, search for Shortcode and drag the widget onto the page.

CSS Custom Properties

The Colors panel writes CSS custom properties, and every stylesheet rule keeps its own value as fallback; you can set the same properties from your theme.

Every one of the forty blocks renders inside a wrapper carrying the class bTestimonials, so that is the selector to use when you want one set of values across the whole library:

.bTestimonials {
    --btb-accent: #0b81ee;
    --btb-surface: #ffffff;
    --btb-border: #e6e8f0;
    --btb-title: #101828;
    --btb-body: #344054;
    --btb-muted: #667085;
    --btb-star: #ff8c02;
    --btb-track: #f2f4f7;
}

Each block also carries its own class, so a single layout can be targeted on its own: the block name with the slash turned into a dash and wp-block- in front:

/* One layout only */
.wp-block-bptmb-testimonials-slider { --btb-accent: #16a34a; }
.wp-block-bptmb-google-review-badge { --btb-star: #fbbf24; }
Important

.wp-block-bptmb-b-testimonials is the classic single block and nothing else; it is not a wrapper around the other layouts, so styling it will not reach a grid, a slider or a badge. Use .bTestimonials for all of them.

Troubleshooting

Something looks wrong? Do not worry. Almost every problem here has a quick fix. Find the one that matches what you see.

A Block Is Missing from the Inserter

You open the inserter and cannot find the layout you want. Check this first: the inserter shows only one block, Testimonials. All the layouts live inside it. That is on purpose.

  • You are looking for a layout. Insert Testimonials, then pick the layout from the picker. See Switching Layouts.
  • The layout is switched off. Open Testimonials > Demo & Help > All Blocks and turn its switch back on. See Turning Blocks On and Off.
  • Even Testimonials is missing. Make sure the plugin is active on the Plugins page. Then reload the editor.

A Block Shows Sample Reviews on the Front End

Every layout ships with sample cards so the design is visible immediately; seeing them published means the block is still holding them.

  1. Select the block and open the card editor on the General tab.
  2. Replace or remove the sample cards.
  3. Or set Content Source to Testimonials (CPT), which ignores the cards entirely.
  4. Update the page and clear your site cache.

The Testimonials Source Shows Nothing

You set Content Source to Testimonials (CPT), but the block is empty. Open Testimonials > All Testimonials and look at the list. The answer is usually there.

  • The reviews are still pending. Only published reviews show. Approve them under Testimonials > Submissions. See Reviewing Customer Submissions.
  • The number is too low. Raise Number in the Content Source panel. It starts at 6.
  • The order looks random. Change Order By to Date or Title.

A Style Control Does Nothing

You change a color or a size and nothing moves. Try these, in this order.

  • Clear your cache. Empty your page cache and any CSS or JS cache. Then reload the page.
  • Your theme may be winning. Some themes set their own styles, and those can beat the block. Ask your theme developer, or see CSS Custom Properties.
  • That layout may not use it. Each layout only shows controls it can use. If a control is on screen and still does nothing, tell support the layout name and the panel it was in.

A Submission Never Arrives

A visitor says they sent a review, but you cannot find it. Send one yourself and read the message the form gives back. It tells you the cause.

  • Please provide your name and a review. One of the two required boxes was empty. Fill in both.
  • Security check failed. The page was saved in a cache for too long. Reload the page and try again. Also leave the form page out of your cache.
  • The photo did not arrive. Use a JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP file under 2 MB. The review is still saved without the photo.
  • Sent too quickly, or sent too many times. The form blocks spam. It stops very fast submissions, reviews with many links, and the same review sent twice in an hour. A real visitor almost never hits this. Wait an hour and try again.

A Slider or Marquee Does Not Move

  • Turn on AutoPlay in the Slider panel.
  • For a marquee, check Pause on hover. It stops the movement while the mouse is over it.
  • Pause while editing only stops the editor. It never changes the published page.
  • Add more reviews. A slider with one card has nowhere to go.
  • Another plugin may be blocking the script. Open your browser console and look for red errors.

A Badge Shows the Wrong Score

The number on a badge looks old or wrong. What to do depends on the badge.

  • Google or Facebook, set to Live. The score is saved for 12 hours. Open Testimonials > Review Sources and click Test & fetch now on that card. See Connecting Google and Facebook Reviews.
  • Trustpilot, G2, Capterra, or any badge set to Manual. These show what you typed. Change Rating Score and Review Count in the block settings.
Still stuck?

Write down the block name, the layout, your WordPress version and what you expected to happen. Then post it on the support forum.
