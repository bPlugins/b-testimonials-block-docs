# Final Proofreading Checklist

## Recipe-level checks

- [ ] Every ingredient appears in the directions.
- [ ] Every ingredient named in the directions appears in the list.
- [ ] Ingredient order follows order of use.
- [ ] Pan size, appliance setting, temperature, time, and doneness cue are explicit.
- [ ] Raw versus cooked weight is unambiguous.
- [ ] Yield and serving size match the tested result.
- [ ] Storage guidance is appropriate for the food.
- [ ] Nutrition is marked verified and matches the final tested recipe.

## Book-level checks

- [ ] US spelling, punctuation, capitalization, and measurement style are consistent.
- [ ] Recipe numbering runs from 1 to 120 without duplicates.
- [ ] Table of contents and index match final page numbers.
- [ ] No placeholders, development notes, or unverified claims remain.
- [ ] Disclaimer is reviewed by a qualified professional.
- [ ] Author name and title are identical on cover, title page, metadata, and copyright page.

## KDP print checks

- [ ] Interior is set to the chosen 8 x 10 inch trim size.
- [ ] Bleed choice matches the exported file and all full-page images.
- [ ] Margins and gutter meet the final page-count requirement.
- [ ] All images intended for print are effectively 300 DPI.
- [ ] Fonts are embedded in the PDF.
- [ ] KDP Previewer shows no cropped, overflowing, or blank content.
- [ ] A physical proof copy has been checked under normal lighting.
- [ ] Cover width and spine are regenerated from the final page count and paper type.
