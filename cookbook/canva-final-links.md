## KDP upload sequence

1. Create a paperback title in KDP and enter the metadata from `amazon-kdp-listing.md`.
2. Choose 8 x 10 inch trim, standard color on white paper, no bleed, and matte finish.
3. Upload `kdp-interior-8x10-standard-color.pdf` as the manuscript.
4. Upload `kdp-full-wrap-cover-72-pages.pdf` as the cover.
5. Launch Print Previewer and review every page, margins, spine, barcode placement, and cover alignment.
6. Resolve every warning before approving the preview.
7. Confirm rights and the AI-generated text disclosure accurately.
8. Confirm printing cost, territory rights, and list price.
9. Order a physical proof and correct any production issue before publishing.
