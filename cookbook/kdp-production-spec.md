- Interior PDF page size: 8.625 x 11.25 inches (0.125 inch bleed on all sides)
- 72 pages
- Standard color interior on white paper
- Bleed interior: 0.125 inch on all sides
- Minimum inside/gutter margin for 24-150 pages: 0.375 inch
- Minimum outside margin without bleed: 0.25 inch
- Interior design uses approximately 0.47-0.54 inch content margins
- Cover bleed: 0.125 inch on all outside edges
- Standard-color spine width: 72 x 0.002252 = 0.162144 inch
- Full cover size: 17.412144 x 11.250000 inches
