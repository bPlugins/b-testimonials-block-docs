Important Disclaimer

This guide provides general educational information and meal-planning ideas. It is not medical advice, a treatment plan, or a substitute for individualized guidance from a physician, registered dietitian, pharmacist, or other qualified professional.

GLP-1 medications can affect appetite, digestion, hydration, and food tolerance. Follow your prescribing clinician's instructions, especially during dose changes or when symptoms occur. Seek medical care for severe or persistent symptoms.

The meal frameworks are flexible combinations, not tested recipes. No calorie, nutrient, weight-loss, side-effect, or health outcome is promised. Check food labels and professional guidance for allergies, kidney disease, diabetes, pregnancy, swallowing problems, or other individual needs.

START HERE

Build meals around comfort, not perfection.

Each framework helps you combine a protein anchor, produce, an optional energy food, and a flavor element. Choose foods you tolerate, prepare them using trusted package or food-safety instructions, and begin with a comfortable portion.

1

Pick protein

Use a familiar protein that fits your care plan.

2

Add produce

Choose soft, cooked, or raw texture according to tolerance.

3

Add energy

Include a modest grain, bean, fruit, or starchy vegetable if desired.

4

Finish simply

Use a measured sauce, herb, or seasoning.

PERSONALIZE

Adjust the meal to the day.

When appetite is low

Choose softer foods, reduce the number of components, and save the remainder promptly.

When nausea is present

Follow clinical advice. Mild, cool, lower-fat foods may feel easier for some people.

When fullness arrives quickly

Eat slowly, pause often, and avoid forcing a listed portion.

When fiber feels difficult

Try cooked, peeled, blended, or smaller portions of produce and legumes.

Hydration

Follow individual guidance. Some people prefer fluids between meals.

Medication changes

Tolerance may shift. Contact the prescribing clinician about persistent symptoms.

Approximately 15-30 g

PROTEIN ANCHOR

PRODUCE

soft-cooked spinach or zucchini

OPTIONAL ENERGY FOOD

FLAVOR

PUT IT TOGETHER
