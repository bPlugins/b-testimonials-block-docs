## Decision after pilot

- If 0-3 recipes require major changes: correct them and proceed chapter by chapter.
- If 4-8 require major changes: revise every recipe template before further testing.
- If more than 8 require major changes: stop testing and commission a culinary rewrite first.

## Who should do what

- Recipe tester: cooks exactly as written and records actual yield.
- Culinary editor: rewrites quantities, equipment, timing, and doneness cues.
- Registered dietitian or nutrition analyst: calculates nutrients after final testing.
- Medical/legal reviewer: reviews GLP-1 and weight-loss language.
- US-English proofreader: performs copy edit and final page proof.
