## Description

Smaller appetites call for a more flexible way to plan meals.

The Portion-Smart GLP-1 High-Protein Eating Guide helps readers build simple meals around a protein anchor, produce, an optional energy food, and an easy flavor element. Instead of rigid recipes or one-size-fits-all portions, the guide offers adaptable combinations that can be adjusted to appetite, texture preference, schedule, food labels, and professional guidance.

Inside you will find:

- 48 flexible high-protein meal frameworks
- Breakfast, smoothie, soup, fresh-plate, dinner, vegetarian, snack, and dessert ideas
- Gentler texture and smaller-portion adaptations
- A flexible 14-day planning map
- Shopping and weekly preparation systems
- Eating, tolerance, and care-team worksheets

This is an educational meal-planning guide, not a medical treatment plan or a book of tested recipes. Readers should follow the guidance of their prescribing clinician and other qualified professionals.
