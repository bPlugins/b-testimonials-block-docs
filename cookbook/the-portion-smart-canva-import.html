WELCOME

Smaller meals can still feel complete.

Protein-forward meals can help make modest portions feel more satisfying. This book combines familiar American grocery ingredients, moderate preparation times, and flexible serving sizes.

The repeatable pattern is simple: begin with protein, add produce, include a measured source of carbohydrate or fat, eat slowly, and stop when comfortably satisfied.

How to use this book

  1. Choose a recipe that fits your current appetite and tolerance.
  2. Treat the serving as a starting point, not a requirement.
  3. Eat slowly and pause between bites.
  4. Store the remaining portion promptly.
  5. Adjust spice, fat, fiber, and texture as needed.

Spinach-Feta Egg Cups

This portion-smart recipe pairs eggs and egg whites with spinach and feta in a practical, protein-forward serving.

1
PREP15 minutesCOOK25 minutesTOTAL40 minutesSERVES4PORTION1/4 of recipe

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup (240 ml) egg whites
  • 2 cups (about 250 g) spinach
  • 1/2 cup (about 60 g) feta
  • 1/2 teaspoon fine salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Nonstick cooking spray

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375 F (190 C). Coat a 12-cup muffin pan with cooking spray.
  2. Whisk the eggs and egg whites, then fold in the spinach and feta. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Divide evenly among the muffin cups and bake until set, 18 to 22 minutes.
  4. Cool for 5 minutes. Serve three mini cups per portion.

Storage Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3 days.

Variation Replace the primary protein with a similar lean option or choose a lower-sodium seasoning. Individual tolerance varies, so reduce rich, spicy, or fibrous additions as needed.
