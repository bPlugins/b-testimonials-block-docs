## Required approval gates

1. Culinary edit: rewrite every flagged recipe with recipe-specific quantities, equipment, method, timing, and doneness cues.
2. Physical testing: record actual yield and serving weight for every recipe; retest failed recipes.
3. Nutrition analysis: calculate from the final tested ingredient weights and yield using USDA data or professional software.
4. Medical/legal review: review all GLP-1, weight-loss, tolerance, and disclaimer language.
5. Copy edit and proofread: US English, measurement style, ingredient-direction cross-check, index, and page references.
6. Canva/KDP proof: update the design, export print PDF, run KDP Previewer, and inspect a physical proof copy.
